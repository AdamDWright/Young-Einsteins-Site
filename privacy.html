Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026.

Overview

Young Einsteins ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected through this website, how it is used, and your rights under Australian privacy law.

Information collected through the enquiry form

The contact form on this site collects the parent/guardian name, student year level, mobile number, email address, subject of interest, preferred lesson type, and any comments you choose to include.

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry about tutoring services. It is submitted through Web3Forms and forwarded directly to Young Einsteins' email inbox. The website itself does not store form submissions in any database - there is no backend, database, or admin system behind this site.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand overall visitor numbers and how people use the site (for example, which pages are viewed). GA4 collects anonymised, aggregated usage data and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using browser extensions such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

No sale or sharing of data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information you submit is used only to respond to your tutoring enquiry.

Australian Privacy Act

Young Einsteins aims to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you have questions about how your information is handled, or would like information corrected or removed, please contact us using the details below.

Contact us about privacy

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact young.einsteins@outlook.com.
