Our Programs

Clear structure where it helps, flexibility where it matters.

Students receive support that fits their level, school topics, and goals.

English

English Support

Structured English worksheets and guided support are available from Pre-Primary through to Year 11, depending on student needs.

Students can receive support with reading, comprehension, writing, grammar, spelling, and general English skills.

Mathematics

Maths Support

For Pre-Primary to Year 6, students can follow a structured weekly maths program.

From Year 7 to Year 12, maths support becomes more personalised and topic-based, matching what the student is learning at school.

Science and ATAR

Additional Subjects

Selected Science subjects and several ATAR subjects may be available by request.

Current ATAR support may include English, Applications, Methods, Chemistry, Biology, and Human Biology.

Other subjects may be available depending on the student's needs. Please contact us to discuss.
